NOT gate

The transistor inverts the input. LOW leaves the transistor off and the output HIGH. HIGH turns the transistor on and pulls the output toward RETURN.

LOW → transistor OFF → current flows continuously POWER → LED → OUTPUT and stops at the open transistor.HIGH → base current turns transistor ON → current flows continuously POWER → LED → TRANSISTOR → RETURN.

Yellow animation represents electrical current. In LOW, one continuous path runs from POWER through the LED, across the output node, and along the output wire to OUTPUT. It stops before the open transistor. In HIGH, one continuous current path runs from POWER through the LED, output node, transistor, and RETURN. Base current is separate control flow.
